FAQ

Common questions

How does the AI get the data? +
Our engine leverages Anthropic's Claude API to analyse your digital presence across social media profiles, website, Google rankings, competitor pages, press mentions, and paid media activity — producing a comprehensive report calibrated to Nigerian and African market benchmarks.
How accurate is the data? +
Social media data and website figures are pulled from live analysis. SEO estimates, domain authority, and traffic figures are directional estimates based on industry benchmarks — similar to tools like SEMrush or Moz. Every estimate is clearly labelled in the report.
Can I audit any company, not just mine? +
Yes. The tool works for any company with a public digital presence. Agencies use it to audit prospects before pitching. Consultants use it to benchmark clients. Executives use it to monitor competitors.
What payment methods do you accept? +
We accept cards (Visa, Mastercard), bank transfers, and USSD via Paystack. All transactions are secure and processed in Nigerian naira.
How long does the report take? +
The AI generates your complete report in 30–90 seconds after payment. A loading screen shows you every step in real time. The report is emailed to you immediately upon completion.
Can I implement the recommendations myself? +
The 30-day action plan is written so your internal team can execute it. The Cerebre services section explains what we can handle for you — but it's entirely optional. Many clients use the report as their internal brief.
Is the report confidential? +
Every report is marked Strictly Confidential — Executive Use Only. We do not store or share your report data. It is delivered directly to your email and is not retained on our servers after delivery.

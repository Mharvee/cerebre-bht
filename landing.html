FAQ

Common questions

How does the AI get the data? +
Our engine leverages Anthropic’s LLM API to run live web searches before generating your report. It checks your social media profiles, website, Google rankings, competitor pages, press mentions, and paid media activity in real time so every report reflects the internet as it exists today, not cached data.
How accurate is the data? +
Social media follower counts and website data are pulled directly from live searches. SEO estimates, domain authority, and traffic figures are directional estimates based on industry benchmarks and public data similar to tools like SEMrush or Moz. We label every estimate clearly in the report.
Can I audit any company, not just mine? +
Yes. The tool works for any company with a public digital presence. Agencies use it to audit prospects before pitching. Consultants use it to benchmark clients. Executives use it to monitor competitors.
What payment methods do you accept? +
We accept cards (Visa, Mastercard), bank transfers, and Paystack for Nigerian naira payments. All transactions are secure and processed by Paystack.
How long does the full report take? +
The AI runs 12+ web searches before writing the report this takes 60–120 seconds. A loading screen walks you through each step in real time. The full report is typically ready within 2 minutes of submission.
Can I implement the recommendations myself, or do I need Cerebre? +
The 30-day action plan is written so your internal team can execute it. The Cerebre services section explains what we can do for you if you'd prefer experts to handle it but it's entirely optional. Many clients use the report as their internal brief.
Is the report confidential? +
Every report is marked Strictly Confidential Executive Use Only. We do not store or share your report data. The full report is generated in your browser session and is not retained on our servers after delivery.
